About Us

Welcome to Novapoint Consult Ltd, where innovation meets excellence and transformation is our promise. Based in the vibrant city of Moshi, nestled at the foot of majestic Mount Kilimanjaro in Tanzania, we are a dynamic global consultancy firm with a passion for delivering cutting-edge solutions.

At Novapoint Consult, we believe that every challenge is an opportunity for growth, and every client deserves tailored strategies that propel them to success. Founded by industry luminaries James Kato, Makoye Madata, and Jeremiah Chaki, our journey began with a shared vision: to redefine the consultancy landscape with creativity, integrity, and unwavering dedication.

Our team of experts brings together diverse talents and perspectives, united by a singular purpose: to empower businesses and organizations to thrive in an ever-evolving world. From strategic planning to operational optimization, we offer a comprehensive suite of services designed to unlock potential, drive innovation, and exceed expectations.

What sets us apart is our relentless pursuit of excellence and our commitment to forging lasting partnerships. Whether you're a startup seeking guidance or a multinational corporation charting new territories, Novapoint Consult is your trusted ally on the journey to success.

Join us as we embark on a mission to transform industries, empower communities, and shape a brighter future. Together, let's turn aspirations into achievements, challenges into triumphs, and dreams into reality.

Mission:

Our mission is to drive positive change through bespoke consultancy services that address the unique needs of each client. We prioritize innovation, excellence, and integrity, aiming to create lasting value for businesses across various industries. Our commitment extends to fostering strong relationships and providing strategic guidance that leads to measurable results.

Vision:

Our vision is to become the leading consultancy firm globally, setting the benchmark for excellence and integrity. We aim to empower businesses to navigate complex challenges and seize new opportunities. With a focus on collaboration and client-centricity, we aspire to create a future where every client achieves their full potential.

Our History:

Founded by visionaries James Kato, Makoye Madata, Jeremiah Chaki and Catherine Marisham, Novapoint Consult Ltd began as a small consultancy firm and quickly grew into a trusted partner for businesses, government agencies, and other organizations. We have a rich history of providing expertise in tax consultancy, business advisory, marketing strategies, ICT services, and more. Our rapid growth is a testament to our commitment to quality and client satisfaction.

Our Team:

Our success is driven by a dedicated team of professionals, each bringing unique skills and expertise to the table. Here's a snapshot of our leadership team:

  • James Kato (Managing Director): Oversees business development and operational management with a strategic focus, ensuring the company's growth and success.
  • Jeremiah Chaki (Company Secretary): Specializes in corporate governance, compliance, and legal affairs, ensuring transparency and adherence to regulations.
  • Makoye Madata (Head IT): Leads the development and implementation of innovative ICT solutions, driving the company's technological strategy.
  • Jackline Kimambo (Head of Business): Guides business growth and fosters lasting client partnerships through a client-centric approach.
  • Aron Ashery (ICT Officer): Brings technical expertise and entrepreneurial insights to support the company's ICT projects and innovation initiatives.

  • Innovation: We embrace creativity to develop unique solutions.
